# Newsletter format

Every issue has, in order:

1. **Subject line**: under 60 characters, says what's inside.
2. **Opening**: two or three sentences on the week's theme.
3. **Stories**: 4 to 6, each a bold headline, 60 to 100 words, and a "Read more" link to the source.
4. **One thing to try**: a practical tip readers can act on this week.
5. **Sign-off**: one line.

Style: plain words, short sentences, no jargon the brief doesn't allow. Every claim links to its source. The whole issue reads in under five minutes (about 900 words).
