# Source grading

Give every finding a grade, and keep the link and the date you checked it.

| Grade | Source | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Primary and official: filings, statistics offices, the company's own figures, peer-reviewed papers | Numbers and claims in the report |
| B | Reputable secondary: established news, industry analysts who cite their data | Context; confirm key numbers with an A source |
| C | Opinion, blogs, forums, undated or unsourced pages | Leads to follow up, never evidence |

Two independent sources for any number that matters. If sources disagree, record both and say which you trust and why. Never present a C finding as fact.
